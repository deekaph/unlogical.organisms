About Me


I am just an old nerd who is into nerdy things. Recorded a few hundred songs, produced some audio horror, wrote a couple of books. Hacker. Was a Philosophy nerd in Uni and am still trying to figure this shit out. I love to DJ and work on miniatures and mountain biking and swimming and videos of dogs doing funny stuff. I dislike "social media" and "shareholder value" and billionaires and people who are dicks. Dad to four awesome young adults.

Deliberately flat HTML

This site is deliberately basic ass HTML. There's no dynamic content, no active response bullshit, just oldschool HTML edited in a text editor. This is because I am rebelling against the mainstream web - the clean bullshit corporate homogeneity, the data tracking, the god forsaken advertisements. So I am taking it back to the oldschool, because I am an old fool. If this appeals to you, check out the indie web.. a good place to start is NeoCities.
